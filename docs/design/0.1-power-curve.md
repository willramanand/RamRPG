# 0.1 — Target power curve

Design targets for how player and mob power scale across the level bands. These are **planning targets**
(no scaling code exists yet); WP-6.2 (region level bands) and the economy pass (WP-7.4) tune against
them, and mob content (6.3c) and boss content (6.4c) are balanced to them. Numbers are placeholders
until WP-7.4 does the real tuning pass; treat them as the shape, not the final values.

Anchored to the current baselines: player base `health` 100, base `damage` 5, and the skill XP curve
`50 + 25·level²` (so higher bands take polynomially longer).

| Band | Player level | Target player HP | Target player damage/hit | Mob HP | Mob damage/hit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (starter) | 1–10 | 100–200 | 5–40 | 20–60 | 2–6 |
| 2 (early) | 11–25 | 200–450 | 40–150 | 60–200 | 6–18 |
| 3 (mid) | 26–45 | 450–900 | 150–450 | 200–700 | 18–45 |
| 4 (late) | 46–70 | 900–1800 | 450–1200 | 700–2500 | 45–110 |
| 5 (end) | 71–100 | 1800–4000 | 1200–3500 | 2500–8000 | 110–300 |

Guidance for the WPs that consume this:

- **Time-to-kill** should stay roughly constant within a band (a level-appropriate mob dies in a few
  seconds of focused hits); a mob two bands above the player should be punishing, not instant-death.
- **Region bands (WP-6.2)** carry a `statMultiplier`; the multiplier ladder is chosen in
  `docs/design/6.2-*.md` to land mobs in the ranges above. Elites (affixes, WP-6.1b) and bosses
  (WP-6.4) scale above the band ceiling with their own multipliers.
- **Defense mitigation** is `def/(def+100)`, so defense has diminishing returns; target end-game
  defense should cap effective mitigation well below 100% (e.g. 300 def ≈ 75%).
- Every number here is revisited in the WP-7.4 economy/tuning pass and in `docs/SMOKE_TEST.md`
  scenario 5 (mob band scaling) on a live server.
