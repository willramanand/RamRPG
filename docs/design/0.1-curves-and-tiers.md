# 0.1 — Curves, rarity rules, and tiers

Baseline from `api/skills/Skills.kt` (`XpCurves`), `api/items/RarityRules.kt`, `api/items/Items.kt`
(`Rarity`, `StatRoll`), and the pinning tests `SkillXpCurveTest`, `UpgradeCostTest`, `RarityRulesTest`,
`StatRollTest`.

## Skill XP curve

`XpCurves.polynomial(base, mul)` → `xpToReach(level) = base + mul·level²`. Default skill curve is
`polynomial(50.0, 25.0)` (the ContentOverrides default): reach L0=50, L1=75, L2=150, L3=275.
`XpCurves.linear(base, mul)` → `base + mul·level` (L0=10, L1=15, L10=60 for `linear(10,5)`).

## Item upgrade cost

`RarityRules.upgradeXpCost(from, to) = Σ_{n=from+1..to} (2 + n)` XP levels (0 if `to ≤ from`).
Examples (tested): 0→1 = 3; 0→3 = 3+4+5 = 12; 5→8 = 8+9+10 = 27.
Rarity-weighted: `upgradeXpCost(from, to, rarity) = ceil(base × upgradeCostMultiplier(rarity))`.

## Rarity

`Rarity` = COMMON, UNCOMMON, RARE, EPIC, LEGENDARY, MYTHIC. Per-rarity rules (`RarityRules`):

| Rarity | socketCap | reforgeXpCost | upgradeCap | upgradeCostMultiplier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| COMMON | 1 | 3 | 5 | 1.0 |
| UNCOMMON | 2 | 6 | 7 | 1.5 |
| RARE | 3 | 10 | 10 | 2.0 |
| EPIC | 4 | 15 | 12 | 3.0 |
| LEGENDARY | 5 | 25 | 15 | 5.0 |
| MYTHIC | 6 | 40 | 20 | 8.0 |

`socketCap` and `reforgeXpCost` are tested monotonic-increasing by rarity. Fallbacks:
`socketCap`→1, `reforgeXpCost`→5, `upgradeCap`→5, `upgradeCostMultiplier`→1.0.

## Stat rolls

`StatRoll(stat, min, max)` on an `ItemDefinition`. `ItemInstanceService.rollStats(def, init)` rolls
each stat in `[min,max]` from `init.rollSeed`, so the same seed produces identical rolls
(`StatRollTest`); a fixed range (`min == max`) collapses to that value. This determinism is the basis
for loot seeds (WP-1.1a) and quality scaling (WP-2.1b).

## Material tiers (NOT yet in code — Phase 3 decision)

The roadmap's material tiers (iron → steel → mithril → adamant, with a per-tier stat multiplier such
as 2×/4×/8×/16×) are **not implemented**. They are designed and shipped in **WP-3.2a** as
`ItemDefinition`s tagged `materialTier`, with recipes chaining tiers; the exact multiplier ladder is
chosen in `docs/design/3.2a-*.md`, not here. Do not confuse them with `Rarity` (a quality axis) or
`upgradeCostMultiplier` (a cost axis) above.
